# HypeCheck

Concept mock-up of the HypeCheck app for Truman State's Bulldog B.I.T.E. 2026 pitch competition.

HypeCheck is a social network for shoppers where **only receipt-verified buyers can post**, and anyone can ask them questions.

- **Owner threads:** a buyer posts on day 1 and updates the same thread on day 30 and day 90.
- **Hype vs Reality:** every product shows its social media Hype Score next to its day-30 Reality Score and Regret %.
- **Ask the owner:** shoppers ask questions in the replies; only the verified owner answers.
- **Streaks and Helpful votes:** owners keep a daily answer streak; Helpful votes unlock free Premium months.
- **Buy links:** posts link to the store (Amazon, Walmart). HypeCheck may earn a commission; reviewers never do.
- **Founding 100:** the first 100 verified posters get 30 days of Premium free.

> Products, people and numbers on this page are illustrative.

## Demo script

1. Feed: scroll Maya's GlowDrop thread (Day 1 9/10, Day 30 5/10, Hype 9.2 vs Reality 5.8).
2. Tap the post, tap the suggested question "Does it work on oily skin?", then send. Maya answers and her streak goes to 13.
3. Tap **Helpful** on her answer.
4. Tap **+**, then **Scan receipt**, then **Post** to show the verified-post flow and the Founding 100 offer.
5. Profile tab: streak, Helpful answers and the reward progress.
6. **Double-tap the HypeCheck logo** to reset the demo.

For a full-screen view on a phone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
